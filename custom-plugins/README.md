# Live Commercial Blocker Plugins

Plugins can be used with the Live Commercial Blocker browser extension to indicate commercials however you want or do whatever you want when commercials occur!

## Instructions

Build your own plugin or use one of the plugins [myself](#my-plugins) or the [community](#community-built-plugins) built below!

### How to build your own plugin

For developers and aspiring developers!

1. Copy one of the [templates](templates) of your choice
2. Install requirements
3. Modify it to do whatever you would like
4. Run it
5. Open the Live Thread Ticker extension settings popup
6. Choose the corresponding plugin mode, adjust the api/ws settings if necessary, and verify your plugin manifest loads
7. Check the Debug Mode checkbox in additional settings to help with testing
8. Save settings
9. Go to your favorite streaming site and initiate the extension to test it out!

Please open an issue if you notice anything wrong with a template or the extension.

### How to use an existing plugin

Click on desired plugin below and follow plugin specific instructions.

## My Plugins

Overlay Plugins:

- [VLC Over Commercials](examples/vlc-over-commercials) - Automatically play anything you would like from your PC's VLC player over top of commercials
- [Overlay Any Window](examples/overlay-any-window) - Have literally any open window on your PC be shown over top of commercials

Trigger Plugins:

- [Say NO to Commercials](examples/say-no-to-commercials) - Set your own keywords or phrases to trigger blocking commercials or removing the blocker
- [Thumbs Down Commercials](examples/thumbs-down-commercials) - Connect to your webcam and give a thumbs down to block commercials and a thumbs up to remove the blocker
