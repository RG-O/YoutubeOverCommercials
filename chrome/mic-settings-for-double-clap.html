Microphone Selecting Tips

While this extension's double clap detection mode has built in ways to exclude noise from loud TVs, it is recommended that if you are pluging your laptop into your TV and choose to use your laptop micophone, try to place your laptop as far away from the TV as you can or use an external microphone placed a decent distance from the TV. This can help with your experience, although not completely necessary. If your external mic has a fuzzy cover over it, take it off.
