Configure Your Microphone

You will have to grant the extension access to your microphone in order to use the double clap detection mode. Make sure to select "Allow while visiting the site" to avoid interuptions later.

Mic Selecting Tips

While this extension's double clap detection mode has built in ways to exclude noise from loud TVs, it is recommended that if you are pluging your laptop into your TV and choose to use your laptop micophone, try to place your laptop as far away from the TV as you can or use an external microphone placed a decent distance from the TV. This can help with your experience, although not completely necessary.
